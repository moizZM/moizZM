## Project overview

Mechanical design work for a smart wearable developed during my internship and working student role at microsynetics GmbH. The work included housing design, sensor and connector cutouts, flexible PCB fit checks, PCB and battery holder concepts, CAD section views, 3D printing and prototype assembly checks.

## Problem

Design and improve mechanical parts for a next generation smart wearable while fitting the PCB, battery, sensors, connectors and other components inside a compact housing.

## My contribution

Designed and modified housing parts in Fusion 360. Created cutouts for sensors and connector access. Worked with curved surfaces and internal geometry. Designed PCB and battery holder concepts. Checked flexible PCB placement and internal component fit. Used section views to inspect the complete assembly. Supported 3D printing and prototype assembly checks.

## Validation and result

Checked the complete assembly using CAD section views and physical prototype fit checks. Verified PCB, battery, connector and sensor placement inside the housing. Supported 3D printed prototype assembly and checked the mechanical fit of the parts.
