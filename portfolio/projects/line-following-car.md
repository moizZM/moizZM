# Autonomous Line-Following Car

[← All projects](../README.md#line-following-car) · [Profile](../../README.md#projects-to-start-with)

**Status:** Completed university project

Combined Arduino control with IR, ultrasonic and colour sensors, DC motors and a motor driver.

<p align="center"><img src="../../assets/portfolio/line-following-car.jpg" alt="Autonomous Line-Following Car — project visual" width="520" /></p>

## Problem

Build a vehicle that follows a line, detects obstacles and reacts to colour information.

## My contribution

Designed the chassis, holders and covers in SolidWorks and supported assembly and fit checks.

## Validation and result

Tested line following, obstacle detection, colour detection and motor movement.

## Tools

Arduino · IR sensors · Ultrasonic · SolidWorks · Motors · Prototyping
